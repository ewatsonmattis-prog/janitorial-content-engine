Hi [First Name],

Most cleaning company owners who try cold email give up after two weeks. Not because the channel doesn't work — but because they never had a system. They had a spreadsheet.

Here's the pattern I see constantly: a list gets pulled together, a few emails go out, a handful of replies come in, and then nothing happens. No follow-up. No structure. No booked call. Just silence.

The problem isn't lead generation. It's what happens after someone shows interest.

Office cleaning is a high-trust purchase. Facilities managers and office managers aren't going to hand over a contract after one email. They need to see consistency. They need to feel like you're credible, reliable, and actually worth a conversation. That doesn't happen with a single cold email and a prayer.

What works is a sequence — not just three follow-ups that say "just checking in", but a structured series of touchpoints that build familiarity, demonstrate authority, and make it easy for a prospect to say yes to a 20-minute call.

The cleaning companies winning commercial contracts through cold outreach are doing four things differently:

First, they're targeting a specific vertical — offices, schools, healthcare, or property management — not blasting every business in a postcode.

Second, they're leading with relevance. Not "we offer competitive rates" but something specific to the prospect's situation.

Third, they're following up five to eight times across email and LinkedIn, with varied messages, over four to six weeks.

Fourth, they're tracking everything. Open rates, reply rates, conversion to call. If something isn't converting, they change it.

Generating interest from office managers and facilities teams is achievable. Converting that interest into recurring contracts requires a system that follows up, builds trust, and creates enough familiarity that booking a call feels like the obvious next step.

If your current outreach isn't producing booked conversations consistently, it's worth looking at the process — not just the list.
