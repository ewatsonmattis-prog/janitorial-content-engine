# Email Newsletter: Why your cold emails aren't winning contracts

**Subject Line:** Why your cold emails aren't winning contracts
**Preview Text:** It's not the list. It's what happens after the first email.
**Word Count:** 318

---

Hi [First Name],

Most cleaning companies sending cold emails are doing the same thing: one email, no reply, move on.

That's not a lead generation system. That's a lottery.

The problem isn't your list. Facilities managers, office managers, and property teams are reachable. The problem is that a single cold email from a cleaning company they've never heard of gives them zero reason to respond — and no follow-up means you've handed the contract to whoever stayed in the conversation longer.

Here's what actually works.

The cleaning companies winning recurring office contracts through cold outreach aren't sending better first emails. They're building short sequences that do three things: establish credibility, demonstrate relevance, and create a low-friction reason to reply. That might be five touchpoints over three weeks. It might be a direct question about their current contract renewal date. It's rarely a single shot in the dark.

Trust is built in the follow-up, not the opener.

When a facilities manager sees your name twice, three times, four times — with useful content, a relevant case study, or a specific question about their site — you stop looking like a cold pitch and start looking like a company worth talking to. That's when booked calls happen. That's when contracts move.

The other piece most cleaning businesses skip is targeting. Sending to every business in a postcode isn't targeting — it's noise. Segmenting by sector, contract size, or renewal window means your message lands with the right person at the right moment. A school procurement lead and a serviced office manager have completely different pain points. Treat them differently.

A properly built cold email system — with the right sequences, the right targeting, and consistent follow-up — doesn't just generate interest. It fills your pipeline with qualified conversations that convert.

If your current outreach isn't producing booked calls, the system needs fixing, not just the subject line.
