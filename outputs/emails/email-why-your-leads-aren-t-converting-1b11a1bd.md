Hi [First Name],

Most commercial cleaning companies don't have a lead problem. They have a system problem.

You've probably tried something — a directory listing, a Google ad, maybe even a cold email campaign. You got some interest. A few enquiries. Then nothing. The prospect went quiet, you moved on, and the contract went to someone else.

That's not bad luck. That's what happens when there's no system behind the outreach.

Here's what actually wins office cleaning contracts at scale: trust built over time, not a single cold email hoping for a yes. Facilities managers and office managers don't switch cleaning contractors on a whim. They switch when the timing is right and when one company has stayed visible long enough to feel like the obvious choice.

That means your cold outreach needs to do three things well. First, it needs to reach the right people — decision-makers at companies that are the right size, in the right sectors, in your service area. Second, it needs a follow-up sequence that runs automatically, so no warm lead goes cold because you were busy quoting another job. Third, it needs to build credibility at every touchpoint — case studies, social proof, specific results — so that by the time someone replies, they already trust you.

Most cleaning companies skip all three. They send one email, wait a week, and assume it didn't work.

The businesses winning recurring commercial contracts right now aren't necessarily better at cleaning. They're better at staying in front of the right people, consistently, with the right message.

Building that system isn't complicated — but it does take the right structure, the right targeting, and a follow-up cadence that works without you manually chasing every lead.

If your pipeline feels unpredictable — busy one month, quiet the next — this is usually why. And it's fixable.
