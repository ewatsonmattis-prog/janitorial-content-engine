Hi [First Name],

Most commercial cleaning companies that struggle to win new office contracts aren't short on leads. They're short on a system.

Here's what usually happens: you send a cold email, maybe two. No reply. You move on. The prospect forgets you exist. Six months later, they sign a contract with a competitor who stayed in front of them.

The problem isn't your email. It's the absence of everything that should follow it.

Facilities managers and office managers are not sitting at their desks waiting to switch cleaning contractors. They're busy. They have existing suppliers. Switching takes effort. That means your outreach needs to do more than introduce you — it needs to build enough familiarity and trust that when the moment is right, you're the obvious call to make.

That requires a sequence, not a single message. A well-built cold email system for commercial cleaning typically includes an opening email that earns attention, two to three follow-ups spaced across two to three weeks, a value touchpoint that gives the prospect something genuinely useful, and a clear, low-pressure ask for a conversation. Each message serves a purpose. Nothing is filler.

The companies winning recurring office cleaning contracts right now are not sending better one-off emails. They are running consistent, structured outreach that keeps them visible across a decision window that can last weeks or months.

Consistency is what separates a pipeline from a list of names you emailed once.

If your current outreach drops off after the first or second message, you are almost certainly leaving contracts on the table. Not because the prospect wasn't interested — but because you stopped showing up before they were ready to act.

Building this kind of system takes time to set up correctly. But once it runs, it generates booked sales conversations from cold prospects without you chasing every reply manually.
